Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Glow & Grace ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what rights you have.

1. What we collect

We may collect and process the following:

  • Identity & contact data — name, email, shipping address, phone.
  • Order data — products purchased, order value, delivery details.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser, device, pages visited.
  • Marketing preferences — newsletter opt-in / opt-out.

2. How we use it

  • To fulfil and deliver your orders.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form.
  • To send marketing emails where you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To improve our website, products and services.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

3. Legal basis

We process personal data on the basis of contract (fulfilling orders), legitimate interests (improving our services, preventing fraud), consent (marketing) and legal obligation (tax and accounting).

4. Sharing

We share data only with trusted service providers who help us operate the store — payment processors, delivery partners and email providers — under strict data protection agreements. We never sell your personal data.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the store (session, CSRF protection) and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings.

6. Retention

Order records are kept for seven years for tax purposes. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe. Contact form submissions are kept for two years.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Email privacy@glowandgrace.com to exercise any of these rights.

8. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS), hashed passwords and access controls to keep your data safe. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously.

9. Children

Our store is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Contact

Questions? Write to us at privacy@glowandgrace.com or 14 Rosewood Lane, London, EC1 4TR.
