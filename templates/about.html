About Glow & Grace

Clean, conscious skincare born from a stubborn belief in honesty.

Where it began

One kitchen, one formula, one rule

In 2019 our founder Maya began mixing small batches of vitamin C serum in her London kitchen after failing to find a formula that was both effective and genuinely gentle. Friends asked for bottles. Then their friends did. Glow & Grace was born.

We've grown, but the rule hasn't changed: if we wouldn't put it on our own skin, it doesn't go in the bottle. Every formula is built around a small number of clinically proven actives at meaningful concentrations — and nothing else.
